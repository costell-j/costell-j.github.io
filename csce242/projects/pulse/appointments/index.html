Appointments & care

Feel prepared for every appointment.

Use simple checklists and conversation starters to make the most of a healthcare visit -- before, during, and after you go.

Your appointment, step by step

A little preparation can make it easier to remember what matters.

Before you visit

Gather medications, notes, questions, and a symptom timeline.

During your visit

Share changes, ask questions, and take notes on next steps.

After your visit

Review instructions, schedule follow-ups, and track concerns.

Featured condition

Understanding high blood pressure

Blood pressure is the force of blood against the artery walls. Many people have no symptoms, which is why regular screening matters.

Appointment checklist

Questions worth bringing with you

  1. What could be causing these symptoms?

  2. Are these tests, treatment choices, or next steps to consider?

  3. What should I watch for before my next visit?

  4. Who should I contact if something changes?

Bring the basics

  • Photo ID and insurance details
  • A current medication list
  • Your questions and symptoms
  • A trusted person, if helpful
